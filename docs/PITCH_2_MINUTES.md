# 2-Minute Pitch

**0:00–0:20 — Problem**

"Imagine your finance team has hundreds of purchase invoices and a GST filing to reconcile. Finding a mismatch is only half the job. Someone still has to figure out why it happened, how important it is, and which supplier needs to be chased."

**0:20–0:40 — Pain**

"That creates repetitive spreadsheet work and, more importantly, turns a reconciliation report into another task list."

**0:40–0:55 — Solution**

"We built GST Reconcile — from reconciliation to resolution. It compares the purchase register with filing data, classifies exceptions, calculates the exposure associated with those records, and turns them into a prioritized supplier chase queue."

**0:55–1:35 — Live demo**

"Here is our demo dataset. One click runs the reconciliation. At the top we can immediately see invoices reviewed, matches, exceptions and exception exposure. But this is the important part: the supplier chase queue. Instead of asking a finance person to inspect every row, we show who needs attention first."

"I'll open this exception. We can see the supplier, invoice, priority, supplied values and the reason for the exception. And with one click we can generate the supplier follow-up message."

**1:35–1:50 — Reliability**

"We deliberately do not use AI to perform the financial math. Matching, tolerance checks and exposure calculations are deterministic. AI-style assistance is only used for explanation and communication, with a human still reviewing the action."

**1:50–2:00 — Close**

"Our business model is a subscription for businesses and accounting firms, with pricing to validate. GST reconciliation shouldn't end with finding mismatches. It should tell you what happened and who needs attention. GST Reconcile — from reconciliation to resolution."
